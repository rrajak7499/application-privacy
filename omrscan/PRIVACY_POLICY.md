# Privacy Policy for OMR Scanner

**Effective Date:** October 24, 2024  
**Application ID:** `com.omrscan`

---

### Overview

**OMR Scanner** is designed with your privacy as a top priority. All optical mark recognition (OMR) processing, sheet alignment, and grading are performed **100% locally on your device**. No photos, camera feeds, student names, or exam results are ever uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect

OMR Scanner operates offline for core functionality. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or harvest any personal data, including:

* Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as name, email address, phone number, or address.
* Photos, camera feeds, or images of scanned answer sheets.
* Student labels, test scores, or custom answer key data.
* Device IDs, IP addresses, location data, or network information.

---

### 2. On-Device Storage & Processing

All data processed by OMR Scanner remains securely on your device:

* **Camera & Photo Access:** Camera access is used strictly in real-time to capture photos of OMR answer sheets. Selected images from your gallery are loaded directly into memory for local evaluation.
* **Local Database:** Custom answer keys and grading history are stored in private local application storage on your device.
* **Data Export:** When you choose to export results (e.g., CSV export), the exported file is generated locally and handled exclusively through Android's system file provider and share sheet under your direct control.

---

### 3. Device Permissions

OMR Scanner requests the following permissions solely to provide core features:

* **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Required to take live photos of optical mark answer sheets for automatic alignment and grading.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

OMR Scanner does not incorporate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or cloud storage services. Your interactions within the application are completely private.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for educational environments and users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, OMR Scanner fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

### 6. Security

Since all data remains stored locally within Android's sandboxed application environment, your data security is safeguarded by your device's built-in operating system security measures.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy page.

---

### 8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official Google Play Store listing support email.
