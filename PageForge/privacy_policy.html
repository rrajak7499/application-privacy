Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the PageForge mobile application (the "Application").

100% Local & Offline Document Privacy: PageForge processes all your PDF documents, images, text, and signatures locally on your device. We do NOT upload, store, or transmit your files or personal documents to any external server.

1. Information We Collect and Use

PageForge is designed with a privacy-first architecture. We do not collect or store personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Device Permissions Requested

  • Camera Permission: Required only when you use the "Scan to PDF" tool to capture paper documents. Photos captured are processed locally and never transmitted externally.
  • Storage / Media Files Access: Required to allow you to select PDF documents and images for processing, and to save the resulting files to your device's Downloads directory.
  • Internet & Network State Permission: Used solely by third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) to fetch and display advertisements, and to verify network availability.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and technical data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and compile aggregate analytics.

You can learn more about how Google uses data when you use PageForge by reviewing Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Retention and Deletion

All documents generated, converted, or edited using PageForge are stored locally in the Application's private storage or your device's Downloads folder. You have complete control over your files and can delete them at any time directly through the app or your device file manager.

4. Children's Privacy

PageForge does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through third-party services, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
